# Bitcoin Price

Shows the spot price of a crypto coin, refreshed every minute.
The price turns green or red against the previous price, and a sparkline of the last 24 prices appears once there are two.

Settings: `coin` (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP or DOGE) and `currency` (USD, EUR or GBP).

Data: the public Coinbase spot price API at `api.coinbase.com`. No account or key is needed.
